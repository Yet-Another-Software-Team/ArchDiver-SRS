\section{Literature Review}
\label{section:literature-review}

Tushar et al.[1] define architecture smells as "indicators of poor quality"
manifesting within source code at an architectural level of granularity.
He explores the association between architecture smells and design smells
in order to find a correlation between smells in different levels of granularity.
He concludes that while architecture and design smells have a general correlation,
no significant link is established between specific design and architecture smells.
Although the paper explores the relation between architecture and design smells,
it does not explore the classification of architecture smells.
\\
